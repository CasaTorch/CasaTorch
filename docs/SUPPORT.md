## Home network planning

Building or upgrading your home's network, cameras and sensors? Get a one-on-one consultation
with the person who built HouseOS:

- Your goals, your house and your budget - Wi-Fi coverage, wired runs, switches and PoE, VLANs for
  cameras and smart devices, NAS and backups, UPS
- Cameras, doorbells, sensors and locks that work well together (and with HouseOS)
- A written plan afterwards: what to buy, where it goes (marked on your HouseOS floor plan), and the
  order to set it up

Book at **gethouseos.com/network-planning**.

## The HouseOS Box

Don't want to set up a computer? Order a **HouseOS Box**: a Raspberry Pi 5 with HouseOS installed,
in a case with a fan and its power supply. Plug it into your router, open `http://houseos.local:8090`
on any screen, and setup starts. Comes with a year of Pro.

Order at **gethouseos.com/box**. Prefer your own hardware? See [HARDWARE.md](HARDWARE.md) - HouseOS
is free on any Pi, NAS, mini PC or Windows PC.
