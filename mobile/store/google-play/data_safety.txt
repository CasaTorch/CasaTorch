Google Play Data safety answers for CasaTorch 0.9.0:
- Does the app collect or share user data? No data collected. No data shared.
- The app talks only to the user's own CasaTorch (self-hosted) and to casatorch.com/api/find (lists CasaTorch boxes on the same internet connection; nothing is stored).
- Alerts: the user's CasaTorch encrypts them end to end; Firebase Cloud Messaging carries sealed bytes only. Firebase Analytics is off.
- Data is encrypted in transit: yes (HTTPS; plain http only to home-network addresses of the user's own box).
- Account deletion: there is no CasaTorch account; signing out removes everything from the phone.
